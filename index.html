Amirreza Sattarzadeh Khanehbargh

M.Sc. student and Graduate Research Assistant · Text Analytics and Bioinformatics Lab (TabiLab), Boğaziçi University, Istanbul

I work on natural language processing and machine learning, centred on language models: how their input units (tokenization) are chosen, how they are adapted and fine-tuned, and what their representations encode. My current research, in the ERC-funded LifeLU project with Prof. Arzucan Özgür, treats proteins as a language and studies tokenization and adaptation in protein language models. I have also recently joined the PARI project, working on hate-speech detection in Turkish and Arabic. PARI is developed by the Hrant Dink Foundation with Boğaziçi and Sabancı universities and funded by the European Union and the Friedrich Naumann Foundation.

Before Boğaziçi I worked on financial NLP at Iran University of Science and Technology, where I was also a teaching assistant for seven course sections, including Head TA for Data Science and Scrum Master in two software-engineering courses. In industry I built speech and text data pipelines for a Persian speech-recognition product.

Research interests

Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML), centred on language models (LMs): tokenization and input representations, efficient adaptation and fine-tuning, and interpretability of what models encode. I am interested in adapting NLP and ML to other domains, from biological sequences to social and financial text.

Publications and posters

  1. CAMDA2026
    Benchmarking Recent Single-Cell Synthetic Data Generation Methods on the OneK1K Dataset Buse Giledereli*, Başar Temiz*, Amirreza Sattarzadeh Khanehbargh* ELSA Health Privacy Challenge, CAMDA 2026 at ISMB 2026, Washington, DC (selected talk and poster) · Preprint, 2026 · *equal contribution
    arXiv Challenge

    We benchmark single-cell RNA-seq generators, including an LLM-based one (Cell2Sentence), for biological utility against membership-inference privacy, and show that how non-highly-variable genes are reconstructed is the main privacy lever.

  4. arXiv2025
    Market-Derived Financial Sentiment Analysis: Context-Aware Language Models for Crypto Forecasting Hamid Moradi-Kamali, Mohammad-Hossein Rajabi-Ghozlou, Mahdi Ghazavi, Ali Soltani, Amirreza Sattarzadeh, Reza Entezari-Maleki Preprint, 2025
    arXiv
